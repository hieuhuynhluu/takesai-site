Get the beta

TakesAI is in beta until it reaches the App Store

Tap Send on your takes. TakesAI cuts the retakes, ums and dead air.

iPhone

  1. Install TestFlight

    Apple's app for beta apps, from the App Store.

  2. Accept the invite

    On your iPhone, open the invite and tap Accept, then Install.

    Join the iPhone beta

  3. Sign in

    Open TakesAI and sign in with Apple, Google or your email.

Android

Open in the US, Canada and Australia. Use one Google account for all three steps:

  1. Join the tester group

    groups.google.com/g/takesai-testers

  2. Opt in to the test

    play.google.com/apps/testing/com.hieuhuynhluu.takesai

  3. Install from Google Play

    Then open TakesAI and sign in.

    Open the Play page

Please stay opted in for 14 days. Google needs that before we launch on Android.

Your plan

Plans open on takesai.app soon. Until then, don't start any trial inside the app.

Start your plan on the web first, then sign in to the app:

  1. Start your plan on the web

    At takesai.app/checkout, with the email you'll sign in with.

  2. Install the beta

    Above, then choose I already have an account.

  3. Sign in with that same email

    Not Apple's "Hide My Email".

  4. Your plan is there

    Don't start a trial or subscription inside the app.

Plan doesn't show? Email support@takesai.app from the address you paid with.

Tell us what breaks

In the app: You → Settings → Report a problem. Or email support@takesai.app.
