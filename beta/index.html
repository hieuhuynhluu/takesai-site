Get the beta

TakesAI is in beta until it reaches the App Store

Film your talking video in takes, tap Send and put the phone down. TakesAI cuts the retakes, ums and dead air and hands back one clean cut, with a receipt of everything it removed.

iPhone

  1. Install TestFlight, Apple's app for beta apps, from the App Store.
  2. On your iPhone, open the TakesAI beta invite and tap Accept, then Install.
  3. Open TakesAI and sign in with Apple, Google or your email.

Android

The Android version is in a Google Play test, open in the United States, Canada and Australia. Use the same Google account for all three steps:

  1. Join the tester group: groups.google.com/g/takesai-testers
  2. Opt in to the test: play.google.com/apps/testing/com.hieuhuynhluu.takesai
  3. Install TakesAI from Google Play: open the Play page, then sign in.

Please stay opted in for 14 days: Google needs that before TakesAI can launch on Android.

Your plan

Plans open on takesai.app shortly. If you have a code or plan to buy on takesai.app, create your account but don't start any trial or subscription inside the app, not even TestFlight's free one: the web plan needs an account that hasn't had one.

To use a code or a plan from takesai.app in the beta, do it in this order:

  1. Create your TakesAI account in the app first. Sign in with your email or with Google. Don't use Sign in with Apple's "Hide My Email": we can't match a hidden address to your purchase.
  2. Don't start any trial or subscription inside the app, not even TestFlight's free one. An account that already had one can't start a trial on the web.
  3. Start your plan at takesai.app/checkout with the same email, and your code if you have one.
  4. Go back to the app. Your plan shows up within seconds.

Paid before you had an account? Email support@takesai.app from the address you paid with, and we'll attach your plan.

Tell us what breaks

In the app, You → Settings → Report a problem. Or email support@takesai.app. Every report gets read.
