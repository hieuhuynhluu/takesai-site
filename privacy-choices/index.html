Your privacy choices

Last updated 13 November 2026 · Applies to the takesai.app website

This website can measure our ads with Meta and TikTok. Here you can turn that off, or back on, for this browser.

Your choice

With JavaScript off, this site sends nothing to ad platforms, so there is nothing to opt out of. The control on this page needs JavaScript.

  • Global Privacy Control
  • Your choice on this browser

Your browser's signal keeps you opted out. To opt back in, turn Global Privacy Control off in your browser's settings.

Opting out reloads this page, so anything already loaded from Meta or TikTok is dropped.

If your browser sends the Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as an opt-out and honour it automatically on every visit, whatever you choose here.

Your choice applies only to this browser on this device. It is saved in this site's storage in your browser, so if you clear this site's data, or use another browser or device, choose again. Global Privacy Control is honoured either way.

What ad measurement does

When it is on, this website tells Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and TikTok when you open a page and when you tap Download on the App Store, the affiliate sign-up or the button that starts a web checkout. With each report they receive the page address, your IP address, details of your browser and an identifier saved in your browser. They use it to measure how well our ads work, and they may connect it to your account on their service.

Some US state privacy laws treat this as "sharing" personal information for advertising, and give you the right to opt out. Opting out changes nothing else: the site works the same.

The TakesAI app

These choices are about this website. The TakesAI app itself has no ad or attribution tracking, so there is nothing to turn off there. What the app collects, and why, is in our Privacy Policy.
