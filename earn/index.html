Questions

Do I need a big following?

No. Anyone 18 or older can join, and approval is automatic.

Do I have to use TakesAI?

Yes, to show your real workflow. Buying through your own link or code doesn't earn commission.

Who gets credit if someone used two links?

A code typed at checkout always wins. Otherwise, the last link they used in the 30 days before buying.

What if a customer gets a refund?

You earn on what they actually paid. A refunded or charged-back payment earns nothing. That's what the 30-day hold is for; if it was already paid out, it comes off your next payout.

Can I share what I've earned?

Yes: your own real results, in your own words, with #TakesAIPartner. Never tell people they'll earn the same, and never promise anyone income.

Where do I see my clicks and commission?

In your dashboard on the affiliate portal, where you signed up.
