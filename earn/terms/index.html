3. How referrals are credited

We cannot credit a purchase it did not record, for example when the buyer cleared their browser, switched devices, or bought without your link or code.   • Commission is earned only when a trial turns into a paid subscription, on each payment as it is made. A free trial, a cancelled trial and free access earn nothing.
  • If the customer changes plan within their first 12 months, you earn 30% of whatever they pay. Payments after the first 12 months earn nothing.
