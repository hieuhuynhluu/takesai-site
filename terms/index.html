4. How edits work

  • One edit is used when a cut first opens in the editor. A basic cut (made without the AI decision, for example when the AI service is down) uses an edit only when you first export it.
  • A failed or cancelled cut never uses an edit.
  • A cut uses at most one edit, however many times you retry it, re-cut it or export it.
  • If a cut cannot be opened, no edit is used. If the app does not report back, an edit is used 24 hours after the cut was delivered to your phone, unless the app reported that it could not open it.
  • If exporting a cut fails twice because of a problem on your phone, the edit is given back automatically, up to 3 times in 30 days. After that, contact support.
  • Deleting a cut does not give its edit back.
  • Edits renew every month from the date your plan started, on yearly plans too. Unused edits do not carry over. Upgrading to a higher plan starts a new month with the new plan's full allowance.
