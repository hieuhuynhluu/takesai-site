Cancel your plan

Where you cancel depends on where you bought your plan.

Bought on takesai.app

Open the customer portal

Enter the email address you bought with. Stripe, our payment processor, emails you a link to your subscription, where you can cancel it.

Or email support@takesai.app from that address and we cancel it for you.

You keep your plan until the end of the period you paid for. If you cancel during a free trial, you pay nothing.

Bought in the App Store or Google Play

Cancel in your phone's subscription settings (how). Deleting the app or your TakesAI account does not cancel a plan.
