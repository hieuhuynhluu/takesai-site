Support

TakesAI by Hieu Luu, a sole proprietor

Contact us

support@takesai.app

In the app, You → Settings → Report a problem sends us your app version and phone model with your note, which helps us fix things faster. It never sends your video or transcript.

A cut failed

A failed cut never uses an edit. The app shows why it failed and what to do; most of the time, tap Retry. If it keeps failing, use Report a problem.

Where is my video?

Your original videos and projects are stored only on your phone; we never receive them. Removing the app removes them too, so export what you want to keep to Photos first.

Edits and limits

One edit is used when a cut first opens in the editor. Edits renew every month and do not carry over. Every plan has fair-use limits, such as videos up to 20 minutes and 2 cuts at a time. The details are in our Terms.

Restore purchases

Signed in on a new phone and your plan isn't showing? Sign in with the same Apple, Google or email account as before, then go to You → Your plan → Restore purchases. Your app store account must be the one you subscribed with.

Cancel your subscription

Subscriptions are managed by your app store. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel one. Cancel at least 24 hours before your renewal date; you keep your plan until the period you paid for ends.

  • iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → TakesAI → Cancel Subscription, or open apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.
  • Android: Google Play → your profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → TakesAI → Cancel, or open play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions.

Refunds

Payments are taken by Apple or Google, so they decide refunds.

  • App Store: reportaproblem.apple.com
  • Google Play: Google Play refund help

Bought on takesai.app?

A plan bought on our website is billed by Stripe, not by an app store, so the App Store and Google Play steps above do not apply to it.

  • Cancel or change your card: go to takesai.app/cancel, or open the customer portal with the link in your receipt email. You can also email support@takesai.app from the address you bought with. You keep your plan until the period you paid for ends. To avoid being charged after a free trial, cancel before the trial ends.
  • Refunds: email support@takesai.app. The rules are in our Terms.
  • Use your plan in the app: download TakesAI and sign in with the email you bought with: your plan is already there. Signed in another way, for example with Apple and a hidden email? Open the link in your welcome email on your iPhone, or enter the 6-character code from that email in the app under You → Settings → Have a web purchase?. An account that already has an App Store or Google Play plan can't add a web purchase; email us and we'll help.

Delete a cut

Delete it in the Cuts library (long-press, or Select → Delete) or in You → Settings → Storage. It is removed from your phone, and its audio, transcript and cut list are deleted from our servers. An edit it already used is not given back.

Delete your TakesAI account

Deleting your account deletes everything we hold for it: your account, cuts, transcripts, audio, consent records and plan history. What happens to each kind of data is in our Privacy Policy. Cancel your subscription first: deleting your account does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription. A plan bought on takesai.app is cancelled at once when you delete your account, with no refund for the time left.

In the app

  1. Open TakesAI and go to the You tab.
  2. Open Settings and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your data on our servers is deleted right away, and the projects stored in the app on that phone are deleted too.

Without the app

  1. Email privacy@takesai.app with the subject "Delete my TakesAI account", from the email address you sign in with.
  2. If you signed in with Apple and chose Hide My Email, or you can't write from that address, say how you signed in. We will ask you to confirm the account is yours.
  3. We delete the account within 30 days of confirming it and email you when it is done.

Privacy and AI processing

Only your video's audio and a list of its frame times leave your phone. When you tap Send, the audio goes to ElevenLabs (or AssemblyAI if ElevenLabs is down) for transcription and the transcript text to Anthropic to decide the cut. Read the Privacy Policy, or email privacy@takesai.app.

Report illegal content or abuse

Email support@takesai.app. See Terms section 8.
