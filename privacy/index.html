Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 November 2026 · Applies to the TakesAI app on iPhone and Android, and to the takesai.app website

TakesAI turns the takes you film into a clean cut. Your video stays on your phone. To make a cut, we send only the sound of the video, and a list of the times its frames appear, to our servers. This policy explains exactly what we collect, who receives it, how long it is kept and how you delete it.

  • Your original videoStays on your phone. Never uploaded.
  • Audio of a video you sendOn our servers until 7 days after the cut is ready
  • Transcript and cutUntil you delete the cut or your account, or after 90 days without use
  • Audio toElevenLabs (AssemblyAI if ElevenLabs is down)
  • Transcript text toAnthropic (Claude)
  • Visits to takesai.appMeasured with Meta and TikTok ad pixels. You can opt out.

1. Who we are

TakesAI is operated by Hieu Luu, a sole proprietor ("we", "us"). We are responsible for the personal information described here. Contact us at privacy@takesai.app.

2. What we collect

Your account

  • Your email address. You sign in with Apple, Google or a one-time code sent by email; we never ask for or store a password.
  • The sign-in identifier Apple or Google gives us, and the account ID we create for you.
  • The name and handle you add to your profile, if any.

Videos you send to be cut

When you tap Send, the app prepares two files on your phone and uploads only those:

  • The audio of the video, as a voice-quality mono copy.
  • A list of frame times: the moment each frame of the video appears, as numbers. It contains no pictures.

With them we receive: the video's length, format and size, the video language and cut pace you chose, where you paused between takes if you filmed in the app, and a fingerprint (hash) of each file so we can check it arrived intact and recognise the same audio sent twice.

What we make from it

  • A transcript: the words spoken and when each one starts and ends.
  • The cut list: which parts to keep and remove, the AI's decision, and a sound waveform the editor draws.
  • A job record: when the cut was sent, its progress, whether it worked, and sizes and times. It holds no audio and no words.

Subscriptions and edits

Your plan, when it started, when it renews or ends, and each edit used or given back. Payment is handled by Apple or Google; we never see your card or bank details.If you subscribe in the App Store or Google Play, payment is handled by Apple or Google and we never see your card or bank details. If you subscribe on takesai.app, payment is handled by Stripe (see "Buying on takesai.app" below).

Buying on takesai.app

If you subscribe on our website instead of in an app store, Stripe takes the payment on its own checkout page.

  • Stripe receives your email address, your card or other payment details, your billing country and postal code (and your billing address where it is needed to work out tax or check the payment), the plan you chose, and the device and browser data Stripe uses to prevent fraud. Stripe also works out any sales tax, GST or VAT for us from your location (Stripe Tax).
  • We receive from Stripe your email address, the plan, its price and dates, your country or postal code for tax, the last four digits of your card and its brand (for example "Visa ending 4242"), and whether each payment worked. We never receive or store your full card number or its security code.
  • Referrals. If you arrive through an affiliate's or partner's link, Tolt, the service that runs our affiliate program, stores a referral ID in a cookie in your browser. If you type an affiliate's or partner's code at checkout, we record the code. When you buy, the referral ID or code is attached to your purchase so the right person is credited. Tolt receives the referral, your email address, and the amount and date of each payment credited to it. The affiliate sees that a sale was credited to them and what it earned, not your payment details.
  • Ad measurement. Starting a checkout is measured by the website's pixels (section 12). When a trial starts or a first payment is made, our server also reports that event to Meta and TikTok, with its value and currency and the details that match it to your earlier visit (an event ID, and the browser identifiers and IP address from that visit), so each purchase is counted once. We do not send these events if you opted out or your browser sent the Global Privacy Control signal (section 12).

If you join our affiliate program, Tolt collects and holds your name, email address, payout details (PayPal, Wise or bank account) and tax forms (such as a W-9), and pays you. We see your name, email address, links and codes, the sales credited to you and what you are paid. The affiliate terms apply.

How you heard about us

When you set up the app, we ask "How did you hear about us?" Answering is optional. Your answer (and a creator's handle, if you add one) is stored with your account. We use it only to count which channels bring paying customers.

Your consent

When you agree to the AI processing screen (section 4), we record which version you saw, the vendors it named, your app version and the time.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, a push token for your phone so we can tell you when a cut is ready or has failed.

Problem reports

If you use Report a problem, what you choose and write, your app version and phone model, and your account ID. Reports never include your video or transcript.

Usage and crash data

Which screens and features you use, crash reports, your phone model, operating system and app version, and an identifier linked to your account. This data never includes transcript text, file names or links to your files.

Technical data

Like any online service, our servers and hosting providers record technical details of each request, such as IP address and time, to run and protect the service.

What stays on your phone

Your original videos, drafts, projects, edits, captions and monthly stats are stored only on your phone. We never receive them. Live captions shown while you review a recording come from your phone's own speech recognition; they are shown on screen only and are never stored or sent to us.

Phone permissions

  • Camera and microphone, to record in the app.
  • Photos: you choose videos with the system picker. On iPhone this needs no access to your photo library.
  • Notifications, if you allow them.

3. How we use it

  • To make your cuts: transcribe the audio, decide what to remove, and send the result to your phone.
  • To run your account and subscription, count your edits and apply the fair-use limits in our Terms.
  • To tell you when a cut is ready, and to answer you when you contact us.
  • To find and fix bugs and understand which features are used, from usage and crash data.
  • To measure how our advertising performs, from visits to takesai.app (section 12).
  • To take payment for plans bought on takesai.app, work out tax, and credit the affiliate or partner who referred you.
  • To count which channels bring paying customers, from your optional answer to "How did you hear about us?"
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse and meet our legal obligations.

In the app, and with everything the app sends us, we do not:

  • sell your personal information, or share it for advertising;
  • track you across other companies' apps or websites;
  • use your audio, transcripts or cuts to train AI models, ours or anyone else's;
  • use face recognition, voiceprints or speaker identification.

The app contains no advertising or attribution software. The takesai.app website does use ad measurement tools: section 12 explains them and how to opt out.

4. AI processing and your consent

Before your first video is sent, the app shows a screen that names each AI company below and what it receives, and asks you to agree. Nothing is uploaded until you do. If we add or change one of these companies, we ask you again.

  • ElevenLabsTurns the audio into a transcript. Receives the audio only.
  • AssemblyAIUsed automatically only when ElevenLabs is unavailable. Receives the audio only.
  • Anthropic (Claude)Reads the transcript and decides the cut. Receives the transcript text only: never audio, video, frames, your name, email or account ID.

Each company works for us under its business terms. It may use what it receives only to provide its service to us and to meet its own legal and safety obligations. Under the settings we use, none of them may train its models on your data.

  • ElevenLabs: we delete the transcript from ElevenLabs after each job. ElevenLabs may keep backup copies for up to 30 days.
  • AssemblyAI: deleted automatically within 1 hour.
  • Anthropic: kept for up to 30 days under Anthropic's standard data retention, then deleted, unless Anthropic must keep it longer under its own policies (for example, to investigate misuse).

You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings. We then send nothing new, so the app cannot make new cuts; cuts you already have keep working on your phone. To remove what we already hold, delete those cuts or your account (section 8).

8. Deleting your data

Delete one cut

Delete a cut in the Cuts library or in Settings → Storage. It is removed from your phone at once, and its audio, frame times, transcript, cut list and AI decision are deleted from our servers. If you are offline, the app finishes the server deletion the next time it can connect. A small job record with no audio or words stays so your daily limits keep counting, and an edit already used stays used.

Delete your account in the app

Go to You → Settings → Delete account. This deletes your account and everything we hold for it: cuts, transcripts, audio, consent records, notification tokens and your plan and edit history. It also deletes the projects stored in the app on that phone, removes you from Superwall and PostHog, and revokes Sign in with Apple. Data on our servers is deleted right away; files of a cut that was still being made are removed within 24 hours. Copies at our AI vendors expire as described in section 4.

Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription. Cancel it in the App Store or Google Play first (see Support). Apple and Google keep their own purchase records under their own policies.

If you bought your plan on takesai.app, deleting your account does not cancel it either: cancel it first (see Support). After deletion we keep only the purchase records that tax and accounting law requires (section 7), and Stripe keeps its own payment records under its own policy.

Delete your account without the app

Follow the steps on our Support page.

Deleting the app

Removing TakesAI from your phone deletes the videos and projects stored in the app, but not your account or the data on our servers. Delete your account first if you want both gone.

10. Children and age

TakesAI is not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If we learn an account belongs to a child under 13, we delete it and its data. If you believe a child under 13 is using TakesAI, email privacy@takesai.app.

11. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections. Our storage is private: each file can be reached only through a short-lived link issued to your own account, and the database lets each account read only its own records. Only our cutting server talks to the AI vendors. Our staff do not listen to or browse user audio; access to production data is off by default and logged. No system is perfectly secure, so we also keep as little as we can, for as short a time as we can.

12. This website

This section is about the takesai.app website only. The TakesAI app contains no advertising or attribution software.

Ad measurement

To see whether our ads and creators' posts bring people to TakesAI, takesai.app uses two advertising tools: the Meta Pixel (from Meta, the company behind Facebook and Instagram) and the TikTok Pixel. When you visit, they receive:

  • the pages you view, and taps on "Download" and on the affiliate sign-up button;
  • the device and browser details and the IP address these tools collect automatically;
  • identifiers they keep in cookies or your browser's local storage, which let Meta and TikTok recognise the same browser later and, if you use their services, connect the visit to your account with them.

Meta and TikTok use this to show us, in totals, how our ads perform. They may also use it to decide who sees our ads, and for their own purposes under their own policies: Meta's privacy policy and TikTok's privacy policy. The pixels never receive your video, audio, transcripts or cuts, or anything else from the app.

Some US state privacy laws call this "sharing" personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising, or "targeted advertising". We are not paid for it.

Opting out

  • Open Your privacy choices and turn ad measurement off. The link is at the bottom of every page.
  • Or do nothing: if your browser sends the Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as an opt-out automatically.

When you have opted out, the pixels are not loaded at all. Your choice is saved in that browser only and is not sent to us, so make it again in each browser and on each device you use, and after you clear the site's data.

Links from creators

A link such as takesai.app/go/name sends you on to the App Store with a tag that names the link you came through. Apple then shows us how many downloads each link brought, as totals. It does not tell us who you are.

Hosting and fonts

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses, and loads its font from Google Fonts.
