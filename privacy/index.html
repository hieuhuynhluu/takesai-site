Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 November 2026 · Applies to the TakesAI app on iPhone and Android, and to the takesai.app website

TakesAI turns the takes you film into a clean cut. Your original video stays on your phone: to make a cut, we send only its sound and a list of the times its frames appear. This policy explains what we collect, why, who receives it, how long it is kept and how you delete it.

  • Your original videoStays on your phone. Never uploaded.
  • Sent to make a cutThe sound and frame times, only after you tap Send
  • Sound of a video you sendDeleted 7 days after the cut is ready
  • Transcript and cutUntil you delete them, or after 90 days without use
  • What the app sends us: sold, used for ads or used to train AINever
  • Visits to takesai.appMeasured with Meta and TikTok ad pixels. You can opt out.

1. Who we are

TakesAI is operated by Hieu Luu, a sole proprietor ("we", "us"). We are responsible for the personal information described here. Contact us at privacy@takesai.app.

2. What we collect

Your account

Your email address, the account ID we create, and the sign-in ID Apple or Google gives us if you sign in with them. We never ask for a password. With Google sign-in we also get your Google name, which the app shows on your profile, and a link to your Google photo, which we keep but do not use. Your profile (a display name and, if you add them, a handle and photo) is shown only to you and, for your handle, on our feature board if you post there. Your phone shrinks the photo before uploading it to private storage; we do not analyse it, and none of your profile is used for ads.

Videos you send to be cut

When you tap Send, your phone uploads two files it makes: the sound of the video, as a voice-quality mono copy, and a list of frame times, numbers with no pictures. With them come the video's length, format and size, the language and pace you chose, pauses between takes filmed in the app, and a fingerprint of each file. From them we make a transcript (the words and their timing), a cut list (what to keep, the AI's decision and a sound waveform) and a job record (times, progress, sizes, your phone model and app version, with no sound or words).

Subscriptions

Your plan, its dates, and each edit used or given back. Apple or Google takes the payment; we never see your card details.Apple or Google takes payment in their stores, and our payment processor on takesai.app. We never see your card details.

Buying on takesai.app

Our payment processor takes the payment on its own page. It receives your email address, payment details, billing country and postal code (and address where tax or fraud checks need it), the plan, and device and browser data to prevent fraud, and it works out sales tax. We keep your email address, the plan, its dates and trial, whether each payment worked, the processor's IDs, any code or referral, when you confirmed it renews automatically, your ad measurement choice (and if you allowed it, your browser's user agent), and the account it is linked to; if you have none, the purchase creates one with the email you paid with. We never receive your card number or billing address.

Referrals. If you arrive through an affiliate's or partner's link, our affiliate platform stores a referral ID in a cookie; if you type their code, we record it. The platform receives the referral, your email address and each payment or refund credited to it. The affiliate sees only a partly hidden email address and what they earned. If you join our affiliate program, the platform holds your name, email, payout details and tax forms and pays you, and we see your name, email, links, codes, sales and payouts. The affiliate termsaffiliate terms on takesai.app apply.

Setup answers

When you set up the app, we ask what kind of creator you are, what you want most, how many videos you post and would like to post, and how long editing one takes you, and, optionally, how you heard about us (with a creator's handle if you add one). We use them to suggest a plan and show the time you could save, and how you heard about us only to count which channels bring paying customers. They go with your usage data to our analytics provider (never the handle). Our subscription screen provider receives your kind of creator, videos a month and suggested plan, and how you heard about us (never the handle).

Usage data and error reports

Which features you use, your phone's operating system, device type and screen size, your app version, your setup answers and your account ID. We do not work out your location from your IP address (usage data from before 16 November 2026 may hold an approximate one). Usage data never includes your name, email address, transcript, file names or links, and we do not record your screen. If something goes wrong, the app sends an error report (the error, its message and where in the code it happened), after your phone removes links, email addresses, file paths and media file names from it.

Other data

  • Problem reports you send: the category, your note, your app version, operating system, the time, your account ID and, from the editor, which cut it is about. Never your video, transcript, edits, phone model or IP address.
  • Notifications: if you allow them, a push token to tell you when a cut is ready or has failed.
  • Earlier AI consents: if you agreed to AI processing in an earlier version of the app, the record of it (the version, the companies it named, your app version and the time).
  • Technical data: our servers and hosting providers log details of each request, such as IP address and time, to run and protect the service.
  • Feature board: if you open Request a feature, its provider receives your account ID, sign-in email and handle, so you are signed in. What you post, comment on or vote for is public under that name once we approve it. The board emails you about requests you follow, and uses cookies and its own analytics. To report a post, use Settings › Report a problem.

What stays on your phone

Your original videos, drafts, projects, edits, captions and stats. Live captions come from your phone's own speech recognition and are never stored or sent. Effects such as Retouch find where a face or person is in the frame on your phone only, and nothing about faces is stored or sent; on Android, the on-device library that does this sends its maker technical diagnostics, never images. The app asks for the camera and microphone to film, photo access only to open the videos you choose (and add-only access to save a cut), and notifications if you want them.

3. How we use it

To make your cuts; to run your account, profile and subscription, count your edits and apply the fair-use limits in our Terms; to tell you when a cut is ready and answer you; to find and fix bugs and see which features are used; to suggest a plan from your setup answers; to take payment on takesai.app, work out tax and credit the affiliate who referred you; to run the feature board; to measure our advertising and give one free cut per person on takesai.app (section 12); and to keep the service secure, prevent abuse and meet our legal obligations.

With anything the app sends us, we do not sell it or share it for advertising, track you across other companies' apps or websites, use your sound, transcripts or cuts to train AI models (ours or anyone else's), or use face recognition, voiceprints or speaker identification. The app contains no advertising or attribution software.

4. AI processing

When you tap Send, the sound of that video goes to third-party AI providers that turn speech into words, and the transcript goes to an AI provider that reads it to decide what to cut. That provider receives the transcript text only: never sound, video, frames, your name, email address or account ID. Nothing goes to them before you tap Send, and your video stays on your phone.

They work for us under business terms: they may use what they receive only to provide their service to us and meet their own legal and safety obligations, and under our settings none may train its models on your data. We delete what they hold after each job, though the speech-to-words providers may keep backup copies for up to 30 days, and the provider that reads the transcript keeps it for a limited time, only to detect misuse. If you don't want your sound sent, don't send videos to be cut: editing and exporting the cuts you have uses no AI.

8. Deleting your data

One cut: delete it in the Cuts library or in Settings → Storage. It leaves your phone at once, and its sound, frame times, transcript, cut list and AI decision are deleted from our servers (if you are offline, as soon as the app can connect). A small job record stays so your limits keep counting, and an edit already used stays used.

Your account: go to You → Settings → Delete account, or follow the steps on our Support page. This deletes everything we hold for it, including the projects in the app on that phone, removes you from our analytics and subscription screen providers and the feature board (your posts stay without your name), and revokes Sign in with Apple. Server data goes right away; files of a cut still being made, or a photo still uploading, within 24 hours. It does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription: cancel that first (see Support). A plan bought on takesai.app is cancelled at once, with no refund for the time left, and we keep only the purchase records tax law requires, no longer linked to you.

Removing the app from your phone deletes what is stored in it, but not your account or our copies.

9. Your choices and rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, for a copy, or to correct or delete it; most deletion you can do yourself (section 8). Turn notifications off in your phone's settings. Opt out of ad measurement on takesai.app on Your privacy choices or with the Global Privacy Control signal (section 12): this is how you opt out of "sharing" and targeted advertising under US state privacy laws.

Email privacy@takesai.app. We confirm the request comes from the account holder and answer within 30 days, and we will not treat you differently for using these rights. If we refuse, reply to appeal: we decide within 45 days and, if we still refuse, tell you how to complain to your state attorney general. You may have further rights under local law; in Canada you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, and in Australia to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

10. Children

TakesAI is not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If we learn an account belongs to a child under 13, we delete it and its data. If you believe a child under 13 is using TakesAI, email privacy@takesai.app.

11. Security

Data travels encrypted. Our storage is private: each file is reachable only through a short-lived link issued to your own account (up to 7 days for your profile photo), and each account can read only its own records. Only our cutting server talks to the AI providers. Our staff do not listen to or browse user audio, and access to production data is off by default and logged. No system is perfectly secure, so we keep as little as we can, for as short a time as we can.

12. This website

To see whether our ads and creators' posts bring people to TakesAI, takesai.app uses the Meta Pixel (Facebook and Instagram) and the TikTok Pixel. They receive the pages you view and taps on Download, the affiliate sign-up and the button that starts a checkout, your device and browser details and IP address, and identifiers kept in cookies or your browser's local storage, which let them recognise the browser later and link the visit to your account with them. Meta and TikTok may use cookies and similar storage to collect information from this website and elsewhere on the internet, and use it to measure and target ads. When a trial starts or a first payment is made on takesai.app, our server also tells them, with a payment's value and currency, an event ID, a one-way code made from your email address and your browser's user agent (no IP address or cookie). They show us totals of how our ads perform, and may use the data to decide who sees our ads and for their own purposes: Meta's privacy policy, TikTok's privacy policy. They never receive anything from the app. Some US state laws call this "sharing" for targeted advertising; we are not paid for it.

Opting out

Turn ad measurement off on Your privacy choices, linked at the bottom of every page. If your browser sends Global Privacy Control, we treat it as an opt-out automatically (we do not respond to Do Not Track). Once you opt out, the pixels are not loaded and no purchase events are sent. Your choice is saved in that browser only, so make it again in each browser and after you clear the site's data.

Links from creators

A link such as takesai.app/go/name sends you to the App Store with a tag naming the link. Apple shows us how many downloads each link brought, as totals, never who you are.

A free cut on takesai.app/start

You can have one video cut for free, with no sign-up. Your browser makes the same two small files as the app and sends only those, with the video's length and a fingerprint of each file, to the same AI providers, kept for the same times. An anonymous account in your browser's storage limits it to one free cut per browser; our bot check confirms you are a person, and we keep a one-way code of your IP address for 24 hours. The page tells our analytics provider which steps you see, the answers you pick and the kind of link you came from, under a random ID that lasts only while the page is open: no cookie, no profile, no location, and never your video, its words or your email. These events are not ad measurement, so they are sent whether or not you opted out.
