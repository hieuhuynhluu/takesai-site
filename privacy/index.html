Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 November 2026 · Applies to the TakesAI app on iPhone and Android, and to the takesai.app website

TakesAI turns the takes you film into a clean cut. Your video stays on your phone. To make a cut, we send only the sound of the video, and a list of the times its frames appear, to our servers. This policy explains exactly what we collect, who receives it, how long it is kept and how you delete it.

  • Your original videoStays on your phone. Never uploaded.
  • Audio of a video you sendOn our servers until 7 days after the cut is ready
  • Transcript and cutUntil you delete the cut or your account, or after 90 days without use
  • Audio toElevenLabs (AssemblyAI if ElevenLabs is down)
  • Transcript text toGoogle (Gemini)
  • Visits to takesai.appMeasured with Meta and TikTok ad pixels. You can opt out.
  • What the app sends us, sold or used for adsNever
  • Used to train AI modelsNo

1. Who we are

TakesAI is operated by Hieu Luu, a sole proprietor ("we", "us"). We are responsible for the personal information described here. Contact us at privacy@takesai.app.

2. What we collect

Your account

  • Your email address. You sign in with Apple, Google or a one-time code sent by email; we never ask for or store a password.
  • The sign-in identifier Apple or Google gives us, and the account ID we create for you.
  • If you sign in with Google: the name on your Google account, which the app shows on your profile, and the link to your Google profile photo, which we keep with your account but do not use.
  • Your profile, shown only to you in the app (and your handle on our feature board, if you post there): a display name (the name Apple or Google gives us, or otherwise the part of your email address before the @, until you change it) and, if you add them, a handle and a profile photo. Handles are unique, so the app can tell someone else that a handle is taken, never whose it is. You take or choose the photo and crop it in the app; your phone shrinks it to a small square image before uploading it to private storage, with no public link. We do not analyse the photo in any way. Your name, handle and photo are not shared or used for ads.Your name and photo are not shared, and none of the three is used for ads.

Videos you send to be cut

When you tap Send, the app prepares two files on your phone and uploads only those:

  • The audio of the video, as a voice-quality mono copy.
  • A list of frame times: the moment each frame of the video appears, as numbers. It contains no pictures.

With them we receive: the video's length, format and size, the video language and cut pace you chose, where you paused between takes if you filmed in the app, and a fingerprint (hash) of each file so we can check it arrived intact and recognise the same audio sent twice.

What we make from it

  • A transcript: the words spoken and when each one starts and ends.
  • The cut list: which parts to keep and remove, the AI's decision, and a sound waveform the editor draws.
  • A job record: when the cut was sent, its progress, whether it worked, and sizes and times. It holds no audio and no words.

Subscriptions and edits

Your plan, when it started, when it renews or ends, and each edit used or given back. Payment is handled by Apple or Google; we never see your card or bank details.If you subscribe in the App Store or Google Play, payment is handled by Apple or Google and we never see your card or bank details. If you subscribe on takesai.app, payment is handled by Stripe (see "Buying on takesai.app" below).

Buying on takesai.app

Stripe also works out any sales tax, GST or VAT for us from your location (Stripe Tax).
  • We keep your email address, the plan and its dates, your trial's length, whether each payment worked, Stripe's IDs for your purchase, any code or referral it came with, when you confirmed that it renews automatically, whether you allowed ad measurement (and if you did, your browser's user agent), the code and a one-way code of the link that add the plan to the app, and which account it is linked to. We never receive or store your card number, its security code, its brand or last four digits, or your billing address: Stripe keeps those.
  • Referrals. If you arrive through an affiliate's or partner's link, Tolt, the service that runs our affiliate program, stores a referral ID in a cookie in your browser. If you type an affiliate's or partner's code at checkout, we record the code. When you buy, the referral ID or code is attached to your purchase so the right person is credited. Tolt receives the referral, your email address, and the amount and date of each payment or refund credited to it. The affiliate sees only a partly hidden version of your email address, that a sale was credited to them and what it earned, never your payment details.
  • Ad measurement. Starting a checkout is measured by the website's pixels (section 12). When a trial starts or a first payment is made, our server also reports that event to Meta and TikTok, with its value and currency for a payment, and what matches it to your earlier visit: an event ID, a one-way code made from your email address, and your browser's user agent. We send no IP address or cookie with it. We do not send these events if you opted out or your browser sent the Global Privacy Control signal (section 12).

If you join our affiliate program, Tolt collects and holds your name, email address, payout details (PayPal, Wise or bank account) and tax forms (such as a W-9), and pays you. We see your name, email address, links and codes, the sales credited to you and what you are paid. The affiliate termsaffiliate terms on takesai.app apply.

How you heard about us

When you set up the app, we ask "How did you hear about us?" Answering is optional. Your answer (and a creator's handle, if you add one) is stored with your account. The answer, never the handle, is also added to your usage profile in PostHog and to Superwall. We use it only to count which channels bring paying customers.

Your setup answers

When you set up the app, we ask what kind of creator you are, what you want most, how many videos you post, how long editing one takes you, and how many you'd like to post. We use your answers to suggest a plan and show the editing time you could save. They are sent with your usage data to PostHog, and Superwall receives the kind of creator you are, how many videos you post a month and the plan we suggest.

Earlier AI processing consents

Earlier versions of the app asked you to agree to AI processing before your first cut. If you agreed, we keep that record: the version you saw, the vendors it named, your app version and the time.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, a push token for your phone so we can tell you when a cut is ready or has failed.

Problem reports

If you use Report a problem: the category you pick, what you write (up to 2,000 characters), your app version, iOS or Android and its version, the time, your account ID and, when you send it from the editor, which cut it is about. Reports never include your video, transcript, edits, phone model or IP address.

Feature board

If you open Request a feature in the app, we send Featurebase, the service that runs our feature board at feedback.takesai.app, your account ID, your sign-in email and your handle (or "TakesAI creator" if you have none), so you are signed in without a second account. Anything you post, comment on or vote for on the board is public under that name once we approve it. Featurebase emails you when a request you follow changes, and each email lets you stop them. Featurebase sets cookies on the board to keep you signed in, and records its own analytics of visits to the board's pages. When you delete your TakesAI account, we delete your profile at Featurebase too; what you posted stays on the board without your name. To report a post, use Settings › Report a problem › A post on the feature board, or write to support@takesai.app.

Usage data

Which features you use (named actions, such as sending a video or exporting a cut), your phone's operating system and version, device type and screen size, your app version, your answer to "How did you hear about us?", your setup answers, and your account ID. No location is worked out from your IP address. (Usage data sent before 16 November 2026 may hold an approximate location, country, region and city, until that profile is deleted.) These events never include your name, email address, transcript text, file names or links to your files, and we do not record your screen.

If something in the app goes wrong, the app also sends an error report: the kind of error, its message and where in the app's code it happened, linked to the same usage profile. Links, email addresses, file paths and the names of video, audio and photo files are removed from the message on your phone before it is sent. Error reports never include your video, transcript or edits.

Technical data

Like any online service, our servers and hosting providers record technical details of each request, such as IP address and time, to run and protect the service.

What stays on your phone

Your original videos, drafts, projects, edits, captions and monthly stats are stored only on your phone. We never receive them. Live captions shown while you review a recording come from your phone's own speech recognition; they are shown on screen only and are never stored or sent to us.

Phone permissions

  • Camera and microphone, to record in the app, and the camera to take your profile photo if you choose to.
  • Photos: you choose videos and photos with the system picker, which needs no access to your photo library on iPhone. If TakesAI asks for access to your library, it uses it only to open or find again the videos you chose, and never uploads them. A profile photo you choose is uploaded only as the small cropped copy described under "Your account". Saving a finished cut to Photos asks for add-only access.
  • Notifications, if you allow them.

3. How we use it

  • To make your cuts: transcribe the audio, decide what to remove, and send the result to your phone.
  • To run your account, your profile and your subscription, count your edits and apply the fair-use limits in our Terms.
  • To tell you when a cut is ready, and to answer you when you contact us.
  • To run the feature board: sign you in, show your requests, comments and votes, and email you about requests you follow.
  • To find and fix bugs and understand which features are used, from usage data and error reports.
  • To measure how our advertising performs, from visits to takesai.app (section 12).
  • To take payment for plans bought on takesai.app, work out tax, and credit the affiliate or partner who referred you.
  • To count which channels bring paying customers, from your optional answer to "How did you hear about us?"
  • To suggest a plan and show the editing time you could save, from your setup answers.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse and meet our legal obligations.

In the app, and with everything the app sends us, we do not:

  • sell your personal information, or share it for advertising;
  • track you across other companies' apps or websites;
  • use your audio, transcripts or cuts to train AI models, ours or anyone else's;
  • use face recognition, voiceprints or speaker identification. Retouch finds where faces are in the frame, on your phone only, to apply its effect; it never identifies anyone, and nothing about faces is stored or sent to us.

The app contains no advertising or attribution software. The takesai.app website does use ad measurement tools: section 12 explains them and how to opt out.

4. AI processing

When you tap Send, the audio of that video goes to the AI companies below, which turn it into words. Nothing goes to them before you tap Send, and your video itself never leaves your phone. If we add or change one of these companies, we update this list before sending anything to it.

  • ElevenLabsTurns the audio into a transcript. Receives the audio only.
  • AssemblyAIUsed automatically only when ElevenLabs is unavailable. Receives the audio only.
  • Google (Gemini)Reads the transcript. Receives the transcript text only: never audio, video, frames, your name, email or account ID.

Each company works for us under its business terms. It may use what it receives only to provide its service to us and to meet its own legal and safety obligations. Under the settings we use, none of them may train its models on your data.

  • ElevenLabs: we delete the transcript from ElevenLabs after each job. ElevenLabs may keep backup copies for up to 30 days.
  • AssemblyAI: we delete the audio and transcript from AssemblyAI after each job. If that fails, we delete them automatically within 2 hours.
  • Google: under Google's terms for the paid Gemini API, Google does not use the transcript to improve its products, and keeps it for a limited period of time, solely to detect and prevent violations of its Prohibited Use Policy.

If you don't want your audio sent to these companies, don't send videos to be cut: editing and exporting the cuts you already have uses no AI processing. To remove what we already hold, delete those cuts or your account (section 8). Questions: privacy@takesai.app.

8. Deleting your data

Delete one cut

Delete a cut in the Cuts library or in Settings → Storage. It is removed from your phone at once, and its audio, frame times, transcript, cut list and AI decision are deleted from our servers. If you are offline, the app finishes the server deletion the next time it can connect. A small job record with no audio or words stays so your daily limits keep counting, and an edit already used stays used.

Delete your account in the app

Go to You → Settings → Delete account. This deletes your account and everything we hold for it: your profile name, handle and photo, cuts, transcripts, audio, consent records, notification tokens and your plan and edit history. It also deletes the projects stored in the app on that phone, removes you from Superwall and PostHog, deletes your profile at Featurebase (the feature board), and revokes Sign in with Apple. Data on our servers is deleted right away; files of a cut that was still being made, or a photo that was still uploading, are removed within 24 hours. Copies at our AI vendors expire as described in section 4.

Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription. Cancel it in the App Store or Google Play first (see Support). Apple and Google keep their own purchase records under their own policies.

If you bought your plan on takesai.app, deleting your account cancels it at once, with no refund for the time left. After deletion we keep only the purchase records that tax and accounting law requires (section 7), no longer linked to you, and Stripe keeps its own payment records under its own policy.

Delete your account without the app

Follow the steps on our Support page.

Deleting the app

Removing TakesAI from your phone deletes the videos and projects stored in the app, but not your account or the data on our servers. Delete your account first if you want both gone.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Access, correct or delete. Ask us what we hold about you, to correct it, to give you a copy, or to delete it. Most deletion you can do yourself (section 8).
  • AI processing happens only for videos you send to be cut (section 4).
  • Notifications: turn them off in your phone's settings.
  • Opt out of ad measurement on takesai.app on the Your privacy choices page, or by turning on the Global Privacy Control signal in your browser (section 12). This is how you opt out of the "sharing" of personal information, and of targeted advertising, under US state privacy laws.

Email privacy@takesai.app. We will confirm the request comes from the account holder, then answer within 30 days. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

If we refuse your request, reply to our answer to appeal. We will decide within 45 days and, if we still refuse, tell you how to complain to your state attorney general.

Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local law. In Canada you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, and in Australia to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner, if you are not satisfied with our answer.

10. Children and age

TakesAI is not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If we learn an account belongs to a child under 13, we delete it and its data. If you believe a child under 13 is using TakesAI, email privacy@takesai.app.

11. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections. Our storage is private: each file can be reached only through a short-lived link issued to your own account (for your profile photo, a link that lasts up to 7 days, so the app can show it quickly), and the database lets each account read only its own records. Only our cutting server talks to the AI vendors. Our staff do not listen to or browse user audio; access to production data is off by default and logged. No system is perfectly secure, so we also keep as little as we can, for as short a time as we can.

12. This website

This section is about the takesai.app website only. The TakesAI app contains no advertising or attribution software.

Ad measurement

To see whether our ads and creators' posts bring people to TakesAI, takesai.app uses two advertising tools: the Meta Pixel (from Meta, the company behind Facebook and Instagram) and the TikTok Pixel. When you visit, they receive:

  • the pages you view, and taps on "Download" and on the affiliate sign-up button;
  • the device and browser details and the IP address these tools collect automatically;
  • identifiers they keep in cookies or your browser's local storage, which let Meta and TikTok recognise the same browser later and, if you use their services, connect the visit to your account with them.

Meta and TikTok use this to show us, in totals, how our ads perform. They may also use it to decide who sees our ads, and for their own purposes under their own policies: Meta's privacy policy and TikTok's privacy policy. The pixels never receive your video, audio, transcripts or cuts, or anything else from the app.

Some US state privacy laws call this "sharing" personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising, or "targeted advertising". We are not paid for it.

Opting out

  • Open Your privacy choices and turn ad measurement off. The link is at the bottom of every page.
  • Or do nothing: if your browser sends the Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as an opt-out automatically.

When you have opted out, the pixels are not loaded at all. Your choice is saved in that browser only and is not sent to us, so make it again in each browser and on each device you use, and after you clear the site's data.

Our website does not respond to the older Do Not Track signal. It honours Global Privacy Control instead.

Links from creators

A link such as takesai.app/go/name sends you on to the App Store with a tag that names the link you came through. Apple then shows us how many downloads each link brought, as totals. It does not tell us who you are.

Hosting

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses.
