    What does TakesAI cut?

    Retakes, ums and dead air. It keeps your best take and adds captions.

    Can I undo a cut?

    Yes, any removed take comes back with a tap. Check each cut before you post.

    What leaves my phone?

    Only its sound and a list of frame times, never the video. Details in the Privacy Policy.

    What is an edit?

    One cut opened in your editor. Failed cuts use none, and unused edits don't roll over.

    How long can a video be?

    Up to 20 minutes.
