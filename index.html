Questions

What does TakesAI cut?

Retakes (it keeps your best take), ums and other fillers, and dead air. It adds word-by-word captions too.

Can I undo a cut?

Yes. The receipt lists everything that was removed, and any removed take comes back with a tap in the editor. Cuts are made automatically and can be wrong, so check each one before you post it.

What leaves my phone?

Your video stays on your phone. To make a cut, the app sends only the sound of the video and a list of the times its frames appear, which contains no pictures. The Privacy Policy names who receives it and how long it is kept.

What is an edit?

One edit is used when a cut first opens in your editor. A failed or cancelled cut never uses one. Edits renew every month and unused edits do not carry over.

How long can a video be?

Up to 20 minutes.

Is there an Android app?

Not yet. TakesAI is on iPhone today, and Android is on the way.

How do I cancel?

In your Apple ID subscription settings. You keep your plan until the end of the period you paid for. Support has the steps.
